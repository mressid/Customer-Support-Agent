# Scrum Events, Artifacts and Working Agreements

## Sprint Planning
Inputs:
- Product Goal and ordered Product Backlog
- latest Increment and feedback
- team capacity, leave, and known operational work

Outputs:
- Sprint Goal
- selected Product Backlog Items
- initial delivery plan

## Daily Scrum
- 15 minutes for Developers.
- Inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal.
- Adapt the plan for the next day.
- Use follow-up conversations for detailed problem solving.

## Sprint Review
- Demonstrate working software, not slides alone.
- Inspect outcomes against the Sprint Goal.
- Invite support staff/admins to test realistic scenarios.
- Capture feedback and reorder backlog based on evidence.

## Sprint Retrospective
Inspect:
- delivery flow and collaboration;
- defect/rework causes;
- CI/test speed;
- architectural friction;
- security/privacy and operational issues;
- one or two actionable improvements for the next Sprint.

## Backlog refinement
Ongoing activity to split, clarify, reorder, and estimate upcoming work. Refinement is not a formal Scrum event.

## Scrum artifacts and commitments
- **Product Backlog / Product Goal:** ordered work and desired future state.
- **Sprint Backlog / Sprint Goal:** selected work and plan to achieve the Sprint Goal.
- **Increment / Definition of Done:** integrated usable result meeting quality requirements.

## Suggested working agreements
- Every story has observable acceptance criteria.
- Security, observability, and automated tests are included in feature work.
- Merge small changes frequently.
- No direct production secrets in source control.
- Do not let model output directly bypass application permissions.
- Review incidents and failed evaluations without blaming individuals.
- Keep story points team-specific; never treat them as hours.
