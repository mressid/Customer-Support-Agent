\chapter{Full Module Catalog}

The module names describe logical ownership. They do not imply one deployable service per module.

\section{A. Customer interaction and conversation}

\subsection{M01 --- Conversation Management}

\textbf{Owns:} conversation lifecycle, turns, participants, message ordering, status, links to cases, and event history. \\ \textbf{Does not own:} provider transport, AI reasoning, or business-system data. \\ \textbf{Core records:} Conversation, ConversationParticipant, ConversationTurn, Message, ConversationEvent. \\ \textbf{Key interfaces:} create/resume conversation; append inbound message; claim/release ownership; close/reopen; retrieve timeline.

\subsection{M02 --- Channel Management}

\textbf{Owns:} channel connections, provider adapters, webhook intake, outbound delivery, provider identities, retries, and delivery state. \\ \textbf{Core records:} ChannelConnection, ChannelIdentity, InboundEvent, OutboundDelivery. \\ \textbf{Key interfaces:} receive provider event; normalize event; enqueue delivery; report delivery status. \\ \textbf{Initial channel:} website chat. Add email and messaging providers after the core flow is stable.

\subsection{M03 --- Customer Identity and Profiles}

\textbf{Owns:} customer profile, contact points, identity linking, preferences, and consent records. \\ \textbf{Core records:} Customer, CustomerIdentity, CustomerContact, CustomerConsent. \\ \textbf{Rule:} a phone number or email address is not, by itself, proof that a person is authorized to access a protected account.

\section{B. AI conversation and voice}

\subsection{M04 --- AI Conversation Engine}

\textbf{Owns:} turn orchestration, intent/context interpretation, policy-aware response planning, model selection, and decision trace. \\ \textbf{Core records:} AgentDefinition, AgentVersion, AgentExecution, ModelConfiguration. \\ \textbf{May request:} retrieval, tool execution, clarification, response generation, or human handoff. \\ \textbf{Must not:} bypass authorization or execute arbitrary model-generated code.

\subsection{M05 --- Knowledge Base and Retrieval}

\textbf{Owns:} source registration, ingestion jobs, parsing, chunking, embeddings, indexing, retrieval, source versions, and source metadata. \\ \textbf{Core records:} KnowledgeSource, Document, DocumentVersion, DocumentChunk, RetrievalRun. \\ \textbf{Requirements:} tenant-scoped retrieval, source freshness, deletion propagation, and traceable citations where supported.

\subsection{M06 --- AI Tools and Business Actions}

\textbf{Owns:} tool registry, schema validation, permission checks, execution, results, idempotency, approvals, and action audit. \\ \textbf{Core records:} ToolDefinition, ToolVersion, ToolExecution, ActionApproval. \\ \textbf{Rule:} model-selected arguments are untrusted. Validate inputs, customer identity, tenant, and authorization before executing.

\subsection{M07 --- Real-Time Voice Sessions}

\textbf{Owns:} live audio session lifecycle, streaming transport, turn-taking signals, interruption handling, timeout, and reconnection. \\ \textbf{Core records:} VoiceSession, VoiceSessionEvent, AudioSegmentReference. \\ \textbf{Transport:} WebRTC is a strong browser-media candidate; WebSockets may suit text/control or some audio-streaming APIs. Telephony requires a separate provider adapter.

\subsection{M08 --- Speech Processing}

\textbf{Owns:} speech provider abstraction, STT, TTS, speech-to-speech sessions, language/voice settings, and speech errors. \\ \textbf{Core records:} SpeechProvider, SpeechConfiguration, TranscriptionSegment, SpeechGeneration. \\ \textbf{Design:} support a modular STT \ensuremath{\rightarrow} AI \ensuremath{\rightarrow} TTS pipeline and optionally a direct speech-to-speech provider behind an interface.

\subsection{M09 --- Transcript and Multimodal Synchronization}

\textbf{Owns:} durable transcript alignment, partial/final transcription state, text/voice message correlation, event ordering, and modality switching. \\ \textbf{Core records:} TranscriptSegment, ModalityEvent, MessageMediaReference. \\ \textbf{Rule:} ephemeral partials/audio chunks should not automatically become durable customer-visible messages.

\section{C. Support operations and automation}

\subsection{M10 --- Human Support Inbox and Handoff}

\textbf{Owns:} agent queues, assignment, human takeover, AI pause/resume, handoff context, internal notes, and handoff outcome. \\ \textbf{Core records:} SupportAgent, SupportQueue, Assignment, HandoffEvent. \\ \textbf{Handoff bundle:} transcript, summary, customer context permitted for the agent, previous tool actions, unresolved questions, and reason for escalation.

\subsection{M11 --- Ticket and Case Management}

\textbf{Owns:} durable support work item, status, priority, category, assignment, comments, due dates, and resolution. \\ \textbf{Core records:} Ticket, TicketComment, TicketStatusHistory. \\ \textbf{Rule:} a conversation is an interaction; a ticket is work to resolve. One ticket may link to multiple conversations.

\subsection{M12 --- Workflow Automation}

\textbf{Owns:} event-triggered business workflows, steps, conditions, schedules, retries, approvals, and execution history. \\ \textbf{Core records:} WorkflowDefinition, WorkflowVersion, WorkflowRun, WorkflowStepRun. \\ \textbf{Rule:} use deterministic workflows for business-critical procedures; AI can classify or propose, while the workflow enforces the procedure.

\subsection{M13 --- Routing, Policies and SLAs}

\textbf{Owns:} routing rules, queue selection, business hours, response/resolution targets, escalation policy, and fallback behavior. \\ \textbf{Core records:} RoutingRule, SlaPolicy, BusinessSchedule, EscalationPolicy.

\subsection{M14 --- Notifications and Follow-Ups}

\textbf{Owns:} operational/customer notification templates, preferences, dispatch, delivery state, and retry. \\ \textbf{Core records:} Notification, NotificationTemplate, NotificationDelivery. \\ \textbf{Boundary:} notification delivery is separate from an ordinary conversational response.

\section{D. Business integrations and insights}

\subsection{M15 --- Integration Hub}

\textbf{Owns:} connector definitions, credentials references, API/webhook mapping, health checks, rate limits, retries, and external event normalization. \\ \textbf{Core records:} Integration, IntegrationCredentialReference, ConnectorDefinition, IntegrationEvent. \\ \textbf{Rule:} credentials are stored in a secret manager or protected encrypted store, never in plain logs or model prompts.

\subsection{M16 --- Analytics and Reporting}

\textbf{Owns:} operational/product metrics, conversation outcomes, channel usage, SLA reporting, voice latency, and cost summaries. \\ \textbf{Core records:} MetricEvent, ConversationOutcome, Feedback, UsageAggregate.

\subsection{M17 --- AI Quality, Evaluation and Improvement}

\textbf{Owns:} evaluation datasets, test runs, quality scores, regression comparison, human review, and release gates. \\ \textbf{Core records:} EvaluationDataset, EvaluationRun, EvaluationResult, AgentRelease. \\ \textbf{Evaluates:} correctness, grounding, retrieval, tool choice, policy adherence, refusal/escalation, latency, and voice quality.

\section{E. Platform administration and governance}

\subsection{M18 --- Organizations, Users and Access Control}

\textbf{Owns:} tenant, memberships, roles, permissions, service identities, invitations, and tenant context. \\ \textbf{Core records:} Organization, User, Membership, Role, Permission. \\ \textbf{Rule:} enforce tenant boundaries in application authorization and data access, not only in the UI.

\subsection{M19 --- Agent Configuration and Administration}

\textbf{Owns:} agent instructions, persona, model/speech configuration references, knowledge assignments, tool allowlist, channel mapping, versions, publication, and rollback. \\ \textbf{Core records:} AgentConfiguration, AgentVersion, AgentDeployment, AgentPolicy.

\subsection{M20 --- Security, Audit, Observability and Usage}

\textbf{Owns:} audit records, retention policy, abuse controls, application telemetry conventions, provider usage, quotas, and usage metering. \\ \textbf{Core records:} AuditEvent, UsageRecord, ProviderUsage, RetentionPolicy, SecurityEvent. \\ \textbf{Note:} billing/subscription management can be a separate module if commercial plans are introduced.

\section{Shared technical capabilities}

These are not necessarily standalone business modules:

\begin{itemize}
\item API gateway / edge rate limiting
\item Background job runner and durable queue
\item Transactional outbox/inbox
\item Object storage for approved attachments and documents
\item Search/vector index
\item Secret manager
\item Configuration and feature flags
\item Structured logs, metrics, and distributed tracing
\item Database migrations and tenant-aware repository patterns
\end{itemize}
